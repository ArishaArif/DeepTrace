What the tool looks at

The project tests one idea: real camera images and generated images may differ in their fine texture.

Fractal texture

Measures how detail repeats across scales inside each patch.

Patch scores

Every patch gets its own score, so one odd region cannot hide inside an average.

An explainable heatmap

The heatmap shows regions contributing to the fake score, so the result is not a black box.

What it cannot do

A screening tool is only useful if its limits are clear.

  • It gives a probability, not proof. Do not use a result to accuse anyone.
  • It works best on photos of faces. Photos of objects and scenes are outside most of its training.
  • New generators can fool it. Our unseen-generator test uses a small sample, so treat it as a rough trend.
  • Compression and resizing change texture. A heavily compressed copy may read as uncertain.

Questions

What can I upload?

Images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) up to 10 MB, and short videos (MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV) up to 50 MB. A clearly visible face works best.

What does the fake score mean?

It runs from 0 (looks real) to 1 (looks fake). Scores near the middle are uncertain, so the tool says so.

Where does my file go?

Your file is sent to the model server to be scored. Do not upload private photos, or anyone’s face without their consent.

Why is a video score a row of bars?

The tool scores sampled moments of the video. Each bar is one moment, so you can see where the score peaks and jump to it.

Screening tool, not forensic evidence. Results can be wrong, especially for photos without faces and for images from generators the model has not seen. Do not use it to accuse anyone.
